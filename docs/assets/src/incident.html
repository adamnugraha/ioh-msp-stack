A problem, from alert to fix

Each tool does its own job, and every step is written to the customer's ticket

1
Zabbix

A problem on a device

High or worse on a managed device, tagged with the customer, the site and the device

2
GLPI

An incident opens

In the customer's own entity, with the due times of the customer's service tier

3
Versus Incident

The NOC chat hears it

After one quiet minute, with the ticket, the Zabbix event and the console page linked

4
MSP Console

An engineer takes it

Acknowledges with a note that reaches the ticket; Investigate drafts the likely cause

5
GLPI

A change is approved

Its category names the playbook and its description the devices; changing templates need this approval

6
Semaphore

The playbook runs

On those devices only; the start and the result, with the log's last lines, go to the change
