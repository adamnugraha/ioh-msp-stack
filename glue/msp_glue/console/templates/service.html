How the figures are made

Availability
Share of the month so far without an open availability problem of severity High or worse on any of the customer's devices. Zabbix calculates it in the service level agreement "Customer <code> availability" that msp-glue keeps for every customer; the target comes from the service tier. A customer added this month starts counting on the first of the month.
Downtime
Time counted as down this month. Hover over it to see how much downtime the target still allows; a negative figure means the target can no longer be met this month.
Breaches
Tickets opened this month that were not taken in charge (time to own) or not solved (time to resolve) within the service level of the customer's tier, as recorded by GLPI. Hover over the figure to see the two counts.
Time to resolve
For tickets opened and solved this month: the average time from opening to solution, in calendar hours.
Compliant and backed up
Active network devices in Golden Config's scope (primary IP address and a supported platform). Compliant: no rule drifted at the last check. Backed up: a successful backup in the last 26 hours.
